BLACK BEARS began with a simple standard: clothing should look sharp, feel right and last. Every piece is chosen for its cloth, cut and stitching, so you can wear it with confidence from the first day.

Our look draws on modern street culture: clean lines, confident silhouettes and pieces that move easily from the city to the dinner table. Collections stay tight and are made properly for men and women who prefer quality over noise.
